## 3. Mathematical Methodology & Emission Factor Lineage

All calculations are implemented in [`backend/app/services/impact_engine.py`](file:///c:/Users/AMEER%20SUHAIL/OneDrive/Desktop/RELIFE/backend/app/services/impact_engine.py) using versioned assumptions (`backend/data/impact_assumptions_v1.json`):

### A. Avoided Carbon ($\text{CO}_2\text{e}$)
Embodied carbon avoidance is calculated from extended operational lifespan:

$$\text{Avoided Carbon } (\text{kg CO}_2\text{e}) = \sum_{i \in \text{Retained}} L_{\text{extension}}(P_i) \times R_{\text{avoided}}$$

Where:
- $R_{\text{avoided}} = 45.0\text{ kg } \text{CO}_2\text{e} / \text{year}$ (annualized displaced new equipment manufacturing carbon footprint per extended year).
- $L_{\text{extension}}(P_i)$ by circular pathway:
  - $\text{Direct Reuse}: +3.0\text{ years}$ ($135.0\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e}$)
  - $\text{Repair / Refurbish}: +2.5\text{ years}$ ($112.5\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e}$)
  - $\text{Secondary Repurposing}: +2.0\text{ years}$ ($90.0\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e}$)
  - $\text{Component Recovery}: +1.0\text{ year}$ ($45.0\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e}$)
  - $\text{Recycling}: +0.0\text{ years}$ ($0.0\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e}$)

**Academic Literature Citations:**
1. *Fraunhofer IZM / Dell Product Carbon Footprint Reports (2021).* Benchmark embodied carbon: laptop ~280.0 kg $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$, desktop ~350.0 kg $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$.
2. *Prakash, S., et al. (2020).* "Timely Replacement or Life Extension of Notebooks: An Environmental Assessment." *Öko-Institut e.V. & German Federal Environment Agency (UBA)*.

### B. Electronic Waste Mass Diversion
Diverted mass is calculated from hardware chassis classification:

$$\text{Diverted E-Waste } (\text{kg}) = \sum_{i \in \text{Retained}} M_{\text{chassis}}(T_i)$$

- Laptop chassis average: $2.1\text{ kg}$
- Desktop tower average: $7.5\text{ kg}$

**Regulatory Citation:**
- *EU WEEE Directive (2012/19/EU) & Basel Convention on the Control of Transboundary Movements of Hazardous Wastes.*

### C. Deferred Procurement Capital Saved
Financial savings are calculated against standard institutional equipment replacement cycles:

$$\text{Capital Saved } (₹) = \sum_{i \in \text{Retained}} \text{Avoided Replacement Cost}(T_i)$$

- Enterprise laptop replacement cost avoided: ₹45,000
- Institutional desktop replacement cost avoided: ₹38,000
- Total savings across 3 canonical demo devices: ₹128,000.

### D. Operational Longevity Extended
$$\text{Extended Lifespan } (\text{Years}) = \sum_{i \in \text{Retained}} L_{\text{extension}}(P_i)$$

- Total life extension across 3 canonical demo devices: $+6.5\text{ years}$ ($3.0 + 2.5 + 1.0$).
- Repaired units add $+2.5\text{ years}$.
- Repurposed secondary infrastructure adds $+2.0\text{ years}$.
- Total operational longevity added across the portfolio: **+60.5 years**.
