## 3. Mathematical Methodology & Emission Factor Lineage

All calculations are implemented in [`backend/app/services/impact_engine.py`](file:///c:/Users/AMEER%20SUHAIL/OneDrive/Desktop/RELIFE/backend/app/services/impact_engine.py) using transparent formulas:

### A. Avoided Embodied Carbon ($\text{CO}_2\text{e}$)
Embodied carbon represents the greenhouse gas emissions generated during mineral extraction, semiconductor manufacturing, assembly, and global transport:

$$\text{Avoided Carbon } (\text{kg CO}_2\text{e}) = \sum_{i \in \text{Retained}} E_{\text{embodied}}(T_i) \times F_{\text{retention}}(P_i)$$

Where:
- $E_{\text{embodied}}(\text{Laptop}) = 247.5\text{ kg } \text{CO}_2\text{e}$
- $E_{\text{embodied}}(\text{Desktop}) = 420.0\text{ kg } \text{CO}_2\text{e}$
- $E_{\text{embodied}}(\text{Display}) = 180.0\text{ kg } \text{CO}_2\text{e}$
- $F_{\text{retention}}$: Retention factor by pathway ($1.0$ for direct reuse, $0.9$ for repair, $0.75$ for repurposing, $0.35$ for component harvesting).

**Academic Literature Citations:**
1. *Teehan, P., & Kandlikar, M. (2013).* "Comparing Embodied Greenhouse Gas Emissions of Modern Computing Devices." *Environmental Science & Technology*, 47(8), 3997–4003.
2. *Prakash, S., et al. (2020).* "Timely Replacement or Life Extension of Notebooks: An Environmental Assessment." *Öko-Institut e.V. & German Federal Environment Agency (UBA)*.

### B. Electronic Waste Mass Diversion
Diverted mass is calculated by device chassis classification:

$$\text{Diverted E-Waste } (\text{kg}) = \sum_{i \in \text{Retained}} M_{\text{chassis}}(T_i)$$

- Laptop chassis average: $2.1\text{ kg}$
- Desktop SFF tower average: $8.5\text{ kg}$
- Desktop mini PC average: $1.3\text{ kg}$
- LCD monitor average: $4.5\text{ kg}$

**Regulatory Citation:**
- *EU WEEE Directive (2012/19/EU) & Basel Convention on the Control of Transboundary Movements of Hazardous Wastes.*

### C. Deferred Procurement Capital Saved
Financial savings are calculated against standard institutional equipment replacement cycles:

$$\text{Capital Saved } (₹) = \sum_{i \in \text{Retained}} \left( \text{Avoided Replacement Cost}(T_i) - \text{Direct Servicing Outlay}(i) \right)$$

- Baseline new enterprise laptop replacement cost: ₹45,000
- Baseline new institutional desktop replacement cost: ₹55,000
- Net savings across 22 circularly retained devices: ₹990,000 (after deducting ₹31,000 in modular repair components).

### D. Operational Longevity Extended
$$\text{Extended Lifespan } (\text{Years}) = \sum_{i \in \text{Retained}} L_{\text{extension}}(P_i, \text{Condition}_i)$$

- Direct reuse adds $+3.0\text{ years}$ of operational service.
- Repaired units add $+2.5\text{ years}$.
- Repurposed secondary infrastructure adds $+2.0\text{ years}$.
- Total operational longevity added across the portfolio: **+60.5 years**.
